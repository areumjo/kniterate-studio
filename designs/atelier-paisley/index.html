Kniterate Studio / product design study 01

Atelier paisley triangle

Detailed floral paisleys, a continuous vine border and room for the pattern to breathe. A first design for a reversible scarf, using the proportions of the Kujten Tafi.

A proper scarf shape

Target: 141 cm along the long edge and 62.5 cm deep. Knit tip-to-tip in principle, keeping one needle boundary fixed and shaping the opposite edge.

Two complementary faces

Midnight and butter are preview colours. The reverse swaps the colours and mirrors the artwork. The stitch draft shows where small details still need refinement.

Before the full knit

Choose the final yarn, measure a washed reversible swatch, then calculate size and shaping frequency. Knit a full-scale motif-and-border section before committing to the whole scarf.
