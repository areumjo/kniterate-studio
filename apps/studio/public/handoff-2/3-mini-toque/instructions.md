## After knitting
1. The top comes off the machine on waste yarn, which holds its stitches. Thread the main-yarn tail through the top stitches below the waste and pull them closed, then unpick the waste yarn.
2. Pull the draw thread (C1) to separate the waste yarn from the cast-on edge.
3. Sew the two long edges together with mattress stitch, one stitch in from each edge, from the crown down to the brim. Where the brim will fold up, sew from the other side so the seam is hidden.
4. Fold the rib cuff up along its middle (the cuff is 19 rows, so fold at row 9); the folded half shows the rib's reverse, which looks the same for 2x2 rib.
5. Weave in the ends. Wash or steam as the yarn label says, then measure.
